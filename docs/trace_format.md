# Recorded intermediate solutions: format

Not distributed (about 15 GB); regenerated by the capture step from the public checkpoints and data. This file documents what the capture step writes.

All twelve intermediate solutions (correction steps) of the released GENCO power-flow checkpoints, recorded with
forward hooks on 44,476 contingency scenarios, together with the AC power-flow reference, DC power flow, and the
per-bus and per-branch tables used in *"Check GENCO Like Newton–Raphson: the Last Correction Step Shows When It Is
Wrong"* (GENCO Challenge 2026).

GENCO itself was not retrained or modified; the model weights are those of `gridfm/genco-pf-contingency` and
`gridfm/genco-pf-datakit`.

## Systems and scenarios

| System | Checkpoint | Per-unit base of the checkpoint (fitted) | Splits | Scenarios |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Texas 2000-bus (`Texas2k_case1_2016summerpeak`) | `gridfm/genco-pf-contingency` (trained on 1–2 outages) | 147.61 MVA | `test_1` … `test_20` (number of outages 1–20): first 1,000 scenarios of each released test split | 20,000 |
| IEEE 118-bus (`case118_ieee`) | `gridfm/genco-pf-datakit`, base, seed 0 (trained on single outages) | 137.93 MVA | `test` (held-out single-outage scenarios of the training data), `nk/k01` … `nk/k10` (generated with gridfm-datakit, 1–10 random branch and generator outages) | 24,476 |
| IEEE 118-bus, robustness | base seeds 1 and 42; small (width 24) seed 0 | 137.64 / 137.88 MVA; small: 137.93 MVA | `nk/k01` … `nk/k10` | 23,904 each |

Outages are uniformly random among in-service branches and generators; elements out of service in every scenario of
a split are not counted.

## Files

Per system and split, `results/<system>/<split>/`:

- `traces/shard_*.npz` — 64 scenarios per shard; all arrays float32 unless stated.
  - `scenario` [S] int64: datakit scenario id;
  - `vm` [S, 12, B]: voltage magnitude per step, p.u.;
  - `va` [S, 12, B]: voltage angle per step, degrees;
  - `res_p`, `res_q` [S, 12, B]: GENCO's own power-balance mismatch per step, MW and MVAr (components fixed by bus type are zero);
  - `loading` [S, 12, E]: |S_ft| / rate_a per directed edge per step (rate_a from datakit's branch table; NaN when out of service);
  - `bus_type` [B] str, `edge_from`, `edge_to`, `edge_branch` [E] int32: directed edge j and j + E/2 are physical branch `edge_branch[j]`.
- `bus.parquet` — one row per (scenario, bus): GENCO's and AC's final |V| and angle, errors, final mismatch, hops to the outage, last-step changes and the other step-wise features.
- `branch.parquet` — one row per (scenario, branch): GENCO, AC and DC loading, error, in-service flag, last-step loading changes, error score.
- `scenario.parquet` — one row per scenario: number of outages, load-scenario id, highest loadings, mean final mismatch, scenario accuracy score.

Per system: `static_bus.parquet`, `static_branch.parquet` (ratings in MVA), and the analysis outputs (`*.csv`).

## Units and conventions

Everything is in physical units: MW, MVAr, MVA, |V| in p.u., angles in degrees, loading = apparent power / rate_a.
Conversion from GENCO's normalised outputs uses each checkpoint's fitted per-unit base (`normalizer_stats.pt`), not
100 MVA. Rows are grouped by `load_scenario` for every resampling: scenarios that share a load scenario differ only in
their outages.

## Reproducing

Recorded with gridfm-graphkit `lfe-tutorial` @ 9d42d82, torch 2.12.1, on one RTX 3070 Laptop GPU. The recorded final
states reproduce the GENCO paper's Texas predictions to float32 precision. See README.md for the commands.
